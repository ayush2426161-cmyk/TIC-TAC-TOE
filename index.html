🎮 HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to Tic Tac Toe! Follow these simple rules and enjoy the game.

👥 1. Two Players

The game is played between two players: Player O and Player X.

🔄 2. Take Turns

Player O starts first, followed by Player X. Players take turns placing their marks.

🎯 3. Winning Rule

Get three identical marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line to win.

🤝 4. It's a Draw

If all nine boxes are filled and nobody gets three marks in a row, the game is a draw.

🔁 5. New Game

Click the NEW GAME button to restart and enjoy another round.
